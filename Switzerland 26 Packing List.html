Switzerland & Northern Italy Packing List

A trip-specific packing list for a 27-year-old female traveler balancing mountain days, cute town outfits, scenic train travel, spa time, and a few bigger adventure days including paragliding, Stoos Ridge, and the Mürren via ferrata.

Trip Style Mountains + towns + transit
Big Conditions Warm valleys, cool alpine mornings, shifting weather
Packing Strategy Layers, repeatable outfits, comfortable shoes
Special Activities Hikes, cable cars, spa, paragliding, via ferrata

How To Pack For This Trip

This itinerary is not a heavy city trip and not a full technical hiking trip either. The sweet spot is a compact wardrobe that works for travel days, nice dinners, mountain villages, photos, and repeated alpine layering. Prioritize comfort, weather flexibility, and easy day-bag access over packing lots of single-use outfits, but still leave room for a few pieces that feel put-together in Sirmione, Lucerne, and Zurich.

Core Travel Essentials

  • Credit cards + debit card

  • Travel insurance details
  • Flight confirmations
  • Hotel confirmations
  • Train tickets and QR codes saved offline
  • Rental car confirmation and pickup details

Phones & Digital Access

  • Phone
  • Charging cable
  • Portable battery pack
  • European plug adapter
  • AirPods / wired headphones
  • SBB app set up and logged in
  • Offline maps downloaded
  • Screenshots of parking passes and reservations

Must-Have Before Leaving

  • Put all tickets in one shared album / folder
  • Download the Tre Cime parking QR code
  • Save the Lago di Braies parking ticket
  • Save the Visp / Zermatt train details offline
  • Share key reservations between both phones

Clothing

These quantities assume a laundry-friendly, rewear-oriented plan rather than packing a brand-new outfit for every day.

Everyday Clothes

  • 7-9 tops total
  • 2-3 lightweight sweaters or long sleeves
  • 2 casual bottoms for travel / town days
  • 1 pair of jeans or easy pants for cooler evenings
  • 1-2 nicer dinner outfits
  • 1-2 dresses / skirts / elevated outfits if that feels more like you
  • 1 easy hotel / lounge outfit
  • Undergarments for 8-10 days
  • Everyday bras
  • 1-2 sports bras
  • 8-10 pairs of socks
  • Pajamas

Mountain Layers

  • Light waterproof shell
  • Midlayer fleece or performance zip-up
  • Packable insulated jacket or warm puffer
  • 2-3 moisture-wicking active tops
  • 1-2 pairs of hiking leggings / pants / shorts
  • Light gloves if you run cold
  • Buff or light neck gaiter optional

Warm-Weather / Lake Items

  • 1-2 lighter outfits for Sirmione / Zurich / Lucerne
  • Swimsuit for Aquaria Thermal Spa
  • Cover-up or easy change layer for spa
  • Light cardigan or layer for lakefront dinners
  • Sun hat or baseball cap
  • Sunglasses

Shoes

Recommended Shoe Setup

  • 1 primary hiking shoe or trail shoe with good grip
  • 1 comfortable walking sneaker for towns and travel days
  • 1 sandal / slide / spa shoe
If your hiking shoe is comfortable enough for general wear, you can get away with just one sneaker-style backup plus spa sandals.

Do Not Forget

  • Break in hiking shoes before the trip
  • Bring blister patches / moleskin
  • Pack a few thicker hiking socks

Best Packing Mindset

Pack for repeat-wearing, layering, and mobility. The highest-value items on this trip are good shoes, a reliable rain layer, a warm layer for altitude, and a daypack that makes trains and mountain days easy. If an item only works with one outfit or one stop, it probably does not deserve suitcase space.
